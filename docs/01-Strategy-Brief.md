# 01 — Strategy brief

## 1. What AIMMI is

**AIMMI prepares Vietnamese business files so they survive foreign scrutiny.**

A Vietnamese business owner considering emigration needs one thing before a programme recommendation: an honest answer to whether their company, their wealth and their documents can withstand the examination any serious process applies. That examination happens in Vietnam, against Vietnamese documents, and it is where these files fail.

Licensed representatives handle immigration processes. AIMMI produces the evidence that makes them possible.

**The destination is a variable.** A client's asset trace, tax reconciliation, ownership evidence and outbound investment filing are substantially the same work whether the file ends in Alberta, an EB-5 regional centre, or Panama. This is the strategic core: adding two destinations required no new services.

### Why it holds

- **Honest about what AIMMI can lawfully do**, which resolves the exposure in the current site rather than managing it
- **The moat is geographic.** No foreign firm can read a *giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp* from abroad and see the amendment date is recent
- **Bills in Vietnam, in VND, on delivery** — revenue does not wait on a nomination
- **Programme-proof.** Five programmes closed or went dormant in eighteen months. A firm selling readiness loses nothing when one does

---

## 2. The audience

Vietnamese business owners, 40–60, who built their companies. Real assets, imperfect documentation. Marketed to constantly and badly. Sceptical of promises, responsive to specifics.

**What persuades them, in order:**

1. Being told something true about their own situation they did not know — the entire strategic basis for the assessments
2. Verifiable credentials — a named representative with a licence number and register link, not a logo
3. Numbers and thresholds — they read financial statements
4. Peer evidence — real cases with permission, no invented statistics

**What does not:** maple leaves, stock families with passports, success percentages, countdown urgency, logo walls of unfamiliar foreign firms.

---

## 3. Positioning against the market

Every competitor sells programmes. When a programme closes, their product line disappears and their content goes stale. Vietnamese search results are full of pages still selling the Start-Up Visa and Ontario's entrepreneur stream.

AIMMI sells file readiness and publishes what has closed. That single page — *Programmes that have closed* — will outrank and outlast every competitor still selling them, and it demonstrates that AIMMI checks.

### The Canada thesis

- Why an operating business, not passive investment — passive investment is banned across every Canadian stream, which most Vietnamese buyers do not know
- Why buying beats building — five jobs implies roughly CAD 1m of revenue, which a start-up cannot reach inside the window
- Which sectors work: food processing, trades, manufacturing, childcare
- The succession wave — retiring owners, no buyers, what that means for a Vietnamese purchaser
- Honest cost ranges

### The destination comparison page

The most valuable content asset in the pack, because nobody selling a single programme can write it. Columns: capital required · capital at risk · active operating role · residency obligation · realistic timeline · what the family gets · who regulates it · **what happens if the programme closes.**

Do not rank the destinations. Disclose the INGWE commercial relationship on the page itself.

## 7. The messaging rule

**"Eligibility" does not appear as something AIMMI assesses, in any language, for any destination.**

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "You qualify for Alberta Rural" | "Your file has the documentation these programmes call for" |
| "You are not eligible" | "Your file is missing evidence every entrepreneur programme requires" |
| "We help with EB-5 eligibility" | "We prepare the source-of-funds file EB-5 requires" |
| "Your score is 112 of 175" | "Your documentation covers 9 of the 12 factors these programmes assess" |

Two reasons. A regulator or complainant reads one company, not four sections — an eligibility claim anywhere undermines the position everywhere. And the exposure is worse on EB-5, where eligibility advice on a US petition is US immigration law practice.

The province matrix and Alberta points calculator are eligibility instruments. They stay internal and are the paid deliverable.

## 9. The homepage

1. **The thesis in one sentence** — not "we help you immigrate." Closer to: *your business file will be examined before you are — here is whether it will hold.*
2. **The first question of an assessment, on the page itself.** Answering one question is the cheapest possible commitment and it starts the flow before anyone decides whether they trust you.
3. **Destinations in brief**, linking to the comparison.
4. **What AIMMI produces**, shown as artefacts — photograph the actual deliverables: a reconciliation memo, a net worth statement, a document index.
5. **Licensed representatives**, named with licence numbers and register links.
6. **What AIMMI does not do**, stated plainly: we do not represent you before immigration authorities, we do not guarantee outcomes, we do not take payment for introductions.

Point 6 is counterintuitive for a sales page and it is the strongest differentiator available. This audience has been promised everything by everyone. Being the only firm that publishes its limits outperforms any claim you could make instead.

## 10. Language

Vietnamese is primary. English is a complete mirror, written second, from the Vietnamese — never the reverse.

**Before any copy is written:** a terminology glossary fixing one agreed Vietnamese term per concept (net worth, source of funds, nomination, licensee, outbound investment certificate, expression of interest, regional centre). Used identically across pages, tools and PDFs. Drift is the fastest way to look amateurish to an audience that reads financial statements.

Rules: sentence case, active voice, plain verbs, no superlatives. The message telling someone they are not ready is the most important copy on the site — specific, unhesitating, no false hope, always pointing somewhere useful.
