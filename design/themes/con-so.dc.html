New direction
01 · Organising principle
The figure. This audience reads financial statements; the site speaks in their native format — ruled rows, code columns, tabular numerals, and one honest number at the centre of every screen. Nothing is illustrated that can be tabulated.
Type: IBM Plex Sans over IBM Plex Mono — one superfamily, so labels and figures share a skeleton and numbers align to the grid; both carry full Vietnamese.
Signature: the result as a metered figure — documentation coverage as a large paired numeral.
Risk: austerity — it can read as an internal tool. The photography rows and generous spacing are the counterweight.
vs banned direction 3 (broadsheet, hairline, dense columns): two-column ledger rows, not newspaper columns; generous row height; a real chromatic accent pair from the brand (navy / orange), not ink-only.
