Theme 2 · Hồ sơ — the dossier

New direction
Organising principle
The paper file itself. Every screen is a page in a dossier: index tabs, document numbers, margin rules, and a verification stamp. The product AIMMI sells is a file that survives examination — the site looks like that file.
Type: Literata (titles and memo text — a serif designed for screens, full Vietnamese) over Public Sans (labels, UI, figures). The serif carries the document's authority; the sans does the administration.
Signature: the result as a stamped verification memo.
Risk: can tip into old-fashioned or legalistic; the generous white page and brand-navy stamp keep it current.
vs banned direction 1 (cream + serif display + terracotta): ground is near-white paper, not cream; the display duty sits with stamped sans labels, not the serif; accent is the brand navy, not terracotta.
