Theme 1 · Bản vẽ — the drawing

Industry design system
Organising principle
The file as a technical drawing under inspection. Every claim sits inside a drawn, registration-marked frame; the result is an inspection sheet. Restraint is structural, not stylistic: nothing on the board is decorated, everything is drawn. Brand applied: the token sheet re-derives from AIMMI navy; the logo’s orange stays reserved for the dots.
Type: Barlow Condensed over Barlow — a condensed engineering title voice above a plain workhorse, both with full Vietnamese coverage and tabular figures.
Signature: the assessment result rendered as a stamped inspection plate.
Risk: technical coolness can read impersonal to a 40–60 audience; the duotoned photographs of real deliverables carry the warmth.
