Theme 4 · Hồ sơ mở — the open dossier

Theme 2, risk mitigated
Organising principle
Still the paper file — held open, not filed away. The dossier grammar stays (pages, document numbers, the verification stamp), but the page breathes: sentence-case serif display, soft corners, and the logo's orange dots living in the interface as the human mark.
Type: unchanged from theme 2 — Literata over Public Sans, both full Vietnamese (same diacritic test applies).
Signature: the verification memo, redrawn as a seal with the dot motif.
Residual risk: softening too far — if the rules and numbers disappear, so does the document credibility. The stamp, index numbers and ruled rows are load-bearing; keep them.
What changed vs theme 2
The dot motif. The logo's orange dots (people) become progress markers, list bullets and section marks — warmth from the brand itself, not decoration.
Sentence case, bigger air. Uppercase administrative labels survive only as small furniture; headings are calm serif sentences.
Soft corners (8px), no hard black rules. The 2px ink rule survives only under the nav; panels are bordered cards, not ruled forms.
Warm photography. Real people and real documents in natural colour, navy-framed — not duotoned, not grey placeholders.
