Shared, in all three

  • Brand applied in all three: AIMMI navy #224185 carries the accent role, the logo’s orange #EB8123 marks gaps and dots, and the logotype replaces the set text brand in every header.
  • The assessment result is the one bold moment; everything around it stays quiet.
  • Tabular numerals wherever numbers appear in sequence; no gradients; no flag palette; body contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
  • Diacritic test string shown per theme at every weight used — all six families ship full Vietnamese subsets.
  • Mockups load Google Fonts for speed of comparison; production self-hosts all fonts (doc 02 constraint). The Industry theme's fonts already ship with the design system.
  • None of the three matches the banned default directions of doc 02 §B3; where one comes near, the walkthrough notes what was changed.
