Destination · Canada

An operating business, not an investment

Passive investment is banned across every Canadian stream. What Canada sells is the chance to buy or build a real business, run it, and be nominated by a province for doing so. Most buyers in Vietnam are told otherwise.

Why buying beats building
Five jobs implies roughly CAD 1m of revenue — a start-up cannot reach that inside the window. An acquired business starts there.
