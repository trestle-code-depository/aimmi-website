The five flows

Flow 1

Assessment flow

Entry to result to optional PDF. The early exit is the most-travelled path and is drawn first, not as an afterthought.

Entry: hub card, or the live Q1 on the homepage
→
Intro screen: what is assessed, privacy line, lastVerified
→
Gate question(s) — may end the flow
↓
Gate triggers — early exit (most-travelled path)
Generous early-exit screen: the honest answer + why
→
One honest alternative direction
→
Leave freely — optional emailed explanation, nothing gated
↓
Profile questions, one per screen, back always available, progress visible
→
Result computed in the browser — no answer ever sent
→
Result on screen, free, ungated
↓
PDF wanted? Email required for the PDF only
→
Newsletter opt-in: separate checkbox, unticked
→
Lead payload: contact + result band + consents. Never the answers
Flow 2

The disqualification path

The most commercially important flow. A visitor told no, honestly and specifically, returns in eighteen months and talks about you meanwhile.

Result: not ready
→
1 · The binding constraint, named plainly, with their own numbers
→
2 · Fixable or not, and roughly how — no false hope
→
3 · One honest alternative direction, as information, not a pitch
→
4 · Optional emailed explanation — last, never a gate
↓
Visitor leaves with a complete answer and no email surrendered
→
sessionStorage cleared. Nothing retained anywhere
Flow 3

Language switching

VI at root, EN under /en/. The toggle preserves the page and, mid-assessment, the progress.

Toggle tapped on any page
→
URL swaps to the mirrored slug — same page, other language
→
hreflang pairs; x-default → Vietnamese
↓
Mid-assessment: current question index kept in sessionStorage
→
Same question re-renders in the other language — progress intact
→
No machine translation anywhere; a missing string fails the build, never falls back
Flow 4

Lead capture and consent

What is collected, what is never collected, and which consent covers which action.

Visitor offers contact: PDF request, contact form, or booking
→
Consents: contact / newsletter / cross-border transfer — each separate, purpose-specific, unticked, in Vietnamese
→
Consent record: timestamp + privacy policy version
↓
Payload allowlist: name, email, phone (optional), language, assessment id, result band, consent flags, timestamp, UTM
→
Cloudflare Worker: validates, rate-limits, rejects any payload with keys outside the allowlist
→
Forwarded to ClickUp. The fact of submission is logged, not the contents
↓
Assessment answers: sessionStorage only, cleared on completion — never in any payload, ever
Flow 5

Destination routing

From the global readiness assessment to a destination page — without the tool ever recommending one.

Readiness result names route TYPES: active business vs passive investment
→
“A licensed representative for your chosen destination determines what you are eligible for.”
↓
Link: compare the three destinations
→
/diem-den — the comparison, unranked, INGWE disclosure on-page
→
Visitor chooses a destination page themselves
↓
Destination page → licensed representative listing → public register link
→
Eligibility conversation happens with the licensee, off-site
