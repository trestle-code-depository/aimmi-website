What AIMMI is, and is not

AIMMI prepares Vietnamese business files so they survive foreign scrutiny. Licensed representatives handle immigration processes; AIMMI produces the evidence that makes them possible.

Scope

What we do
Prepare business evidence, source-of-wealth files, outbound investment compliance, personal documentation, business readiness. In Vietnam, against Vietnamese documents.
What we do not do
Represent clients before immigration authorities. Guarantee outcomes. Assess eligibility — a licensed representative does that. Take or pay referral fees.
